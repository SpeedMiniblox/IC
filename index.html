How to play

1. Tap an element in Your discoveries to put it in the first slot. Tap another for the second slot.

2. Press Make something. The AI will invent a result from the two ingredients.

3. New results are saved, so you can combine them with anything else. You can also add your own custom elements.

Tip: The AI connection must be set up once before AI crafting works. The game itself saves on this device.
